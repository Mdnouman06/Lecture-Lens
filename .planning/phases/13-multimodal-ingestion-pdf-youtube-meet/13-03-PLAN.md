<objective>
Refactor and integrate the frontend multimodal ingestion components in `src/components/ingestion/` and `src/views/IngestionHubView.tsx`:
1. Connect `YouTubeUploader` caption failure handling with seamless 1-click navigation into `ManualTranscriptEditor`, transferring the lecture title and YouTube URL (`INGEST-04`).
2. Add audio transcription failure fallback in `AudioUploader` to switch immediately to manual transcript paste (`INGEST-04`).
3. Document and support the Google Meet recording workflow (`INGEST-08`):
   - Add `.mp4, .m4a, .webm` badges with explicit "Google Meet & Zoom Supported" labeling.
   - Build an interactive "Google Meet Ingestion Guide" modal or inline helper card explaining:
     1) How to download the recorded lecture (`.mp4`/`.m4a`) from Google Drive.
     2) How to drop it into LectureLens for local `faster-whisper` transcription.
     3) How to paste auto-generated Google Docs captions into the Manual Editor.
4. Upgrade `ManualTranscriptEditor` to accept prefilled titles and text, format raw transcripts, and provide real-time word/character counts.
5. Add rich Apple HIG styling and responsive tokens in `src/styles/ingestion.css`.

Purpose: Provide students with a friction-free, foolproof multimodal ingestion experience with instant fallback options and clear Google Meet guidance.
Output: Integrated multimodal ingestion UI with fallback handling and Google Meet workflow documentation.
</objective>
